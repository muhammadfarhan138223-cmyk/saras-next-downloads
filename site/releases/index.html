LATEST PRIVATE PILOT

0.2.0-dev.9

19 October 2026
  • Aegis Urdu speech timings drive six mouth poses across four desktop characters.
  • Emoji symbols are removed from spoken text.
  • Microphone sensitivity, gain and stationary noise filtering controls.
  • Speaker output selection and speech interruption controls.
  • Cleaner captions and configurable character presentation.
  • Timed hamster mouth animation in the web app when relayed speech supplies cues.

Known release boundaries

Other voices do not all expose timing data. Quiet or distant speech and speaker echo need real-device acceptance testing. Public accounts, a signed installer, commercial voice clearance and a public automatic update service remain pending.
