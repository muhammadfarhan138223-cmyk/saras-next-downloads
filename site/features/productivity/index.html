FEATURE GUIDE

Make small tasks easier to keep track of.

Supported productivity tools cover time and date, timers, reminders, clipboard handling and dictation workflows. Use them to capture a thought or send reviewed text to the right place.

Try this

Set a short timer, save a note, or dictate a sentence into a supported text field.

Get it ready

Configure the relevant tool and input permissions. Review dictated text before it is submitted.

What to expect

A sleeping or disconnected laptop can affect reminders. Clipboard text may be sensitive; do not save passwords or confidential content casually.
