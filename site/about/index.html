A more human way to use a computer.

FOUNDER

Farhan Balouch

SARAS Next was founded by Farhan Balouch, with the idea of making everyday computer interaction feel more natural through voice and a desktop companion.

farhanbalouch.com ↗

The idea

A computer can be capable and approachable. SARAS brings conversation, visible feedback and supported actions together without asking users to navigate every setting on their own.

The approach

Make actions visible. Keep permissions understandable. Let people choose the voice and character that suit them. Clearly explain where a feature depends on internet access or Windows permissions.

Where we are

SARAS Next is in private pilot development. The current work focuses on voice reliability, character presentation, companion connection and safe packaging before public distribution.
