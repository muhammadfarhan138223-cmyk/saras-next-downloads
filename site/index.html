WHY SARAS?

Less navigating.
More getting started.

For everyday work

Start small: a note, a reminder, an app launch. Voice and visible transcripts offer another way to reach supported tools.

For learning

Ask for explanations, explore a topic, and request current research when your providers are configured.

For your own workflow

Set a shared workspace, choose your companion, and review computer actions before they happen.

For staying connected

Pair your mobile web app with a laptop that stays online, and keep supported actions within reach.
