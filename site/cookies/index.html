Cookies and storage

Understand browser storage, cookies, analytics and the difference between this information website and the SARAS application.

A small amount of local preference storage

This website remembers your animation preference in browser local storage under saras-site-motion. It stays in your browser and is not sent to SARAS.

No added advertising trackers

This site does not include SARAS advertising, analytics or third-party font scripts. The hosting platform may use its own necessary access or security cookies, especially while the preview is private.

The application is separate

Following a link to the SARAS app opens a separate service. It can use session cookies and stored settings for sign-in and operation. The founder’s website also has its own policies.

Clear a preference

Use your browser’s site-data settings to remove local storage. Your system’s reduced-motion preference is respected even without a stored setting.

Theme preference

The site stores your choice under saras-site-theme. Auto mode uses the Asia/Karachi time zone: light from 07:00 to 18:00 and dark otherwise. It does not read a light sensor, microphone, location or camera.
