Terms of use

Private pilot terms, limitations, user responsibilities and third-party voice model licensing for SARAS Next.

Pilot terms · Updated 19 October 2026.

Current availability

SARAS Next is development software made available for private evaluation. Public pricing, commercial distribution and a general availability date have not been announced.

Use on devices you control

Use the companion only on devices and accounts you own or are authorized to manage. Review actions before approval. Save your work before shutdown, restart, file changes or app closure.

AI and action limitations

AI responses and speech recognition can be inaccurate. A completed request is not a guarantee that every application changed as intended. Verify important outcomes. Do not rely on the assistant as the sole source for medical, legal, financial or emergency decisions.

Third-party services

Cloud providers may require separate accounts and can impose quotas or fees. Availability depends on those services, internet connectivity and Windows permissions.

Voice and software licenses

Some optional voices are personal-use only. The current pilot has not been cleared for commercial redistribution. Applicable open-source notices and voice-specific licenses remain in force.

No production guarantee

The pilot is supplied for evaluation without a promise of uninterrupted service, error-free operation or compatibility with every device. Any future public agreement will need a separate review before registration and sales open.
