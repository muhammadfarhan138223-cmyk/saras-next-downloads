Classic Mouse

The original, with personality.

The original teal-and-pink illustrated mascot stays available. Choose it if you prefer a playful style over photographic fur.

Your desktop, your choice

Choose a character, adjust its size and colour tint, and decide how much it moves. Use corners, screen edges or a fixed place. Blinking, pointer attention, captions and topmost behaviour are configurable.

Visible when you need it

The character can appear for a spoken reply and retreat afterwards. Turn desktop visibility off when you prefer a quiet workspace. Speech animation depends on the selected voice’s timing support.

About these previews

Photographic character poses are presented with animated CSS depth and pointer-facing transitions. Classic Mouse is intentionally illustrated. These previews do not represent a fully rigged 3D model or a physical animal.
